BOTTLENECK · line limiter

Cycle time is how long one workstation or operator needs to complete its assigned work. The slowest one sets the line’s steady output interval.

Find the bottleneck by taking the highest workstation cycle time.
UPH · units per hour

A rate. Compare customer-required UPH with the line’s theoretical capacity.

Required UPH = demand ÷ available hours. Capacity UPH = 3,600 ÷ bottleneck seconds.
Worked check · 30-second takt vs 35-second bottleneck

Demand requires 120 UPH. A 35-second bottleneck supports about 102.9 UPH, so the line falls short by about 17.1 UPH. It cannot meet the required rate until the bottleneck is reduced to 30 seconds or less.

This is steady-state capacity. The shift output below also accounts for the time to complete the first unit from an empty line.
